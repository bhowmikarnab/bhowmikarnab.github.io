About me

I am a Research Scholar pursuing a PhD in Physics at IIEST Shibpur. As a theoretical physicist, I study ultracold quantum gases: Bose–Einstein condensates, dipolar gases and quantum droplets. Using the extended Gross–Pitaevskii equation and Bogoliubov–de Gennes analysis, I explore how quantum fluctuations shape their stability and dynamics.

Alongside research, I teach physics and mathematics to school and undergraduate students, helping them build intuition for difficult ideas.

Research highlights

LHY01

Quantum droplets

Self-bound droplets of ultracold atoms that hold together without a trap. Mean-field attraction pulls the gas inward; quantum fluctuations, the Lee–Huang–Yang correction, push back and stop the collapse.

Bhowmik, Majumder, Banerjee · SSRN preprint 2026 ↗ (opens in a new tab)

εdd02

Dipolar Bose gases

Condensates of strongly magnetic atoms, where long-range, direction-dependent dipole–dipole forces compete with short-range contact interactions. Their ratio, εdd, decides which one wins.

Research interest

|ψ|²03

Extended Gross–Pitaevskii equation

Numerical modelling with the extended Gross–Pitaevskii equation: the mean-field wave equation plus a beyond-mean-field quantum-fluctuation term, used for ground states and real-time dynamics.

Bhowmik, Majumder, Banerjee · SSRN preprint 2026 ↗ (opens in a new tab)

ω(k)04

Collective excitations

Bogoliubov–de Gennes analysis of how a condensate answers small disturbances: its excitation spectrum, phonons and rotons, and when a state stays stable or breaks apart.

Banerjee, Bhowmik, Majumder · Phys. Lett. A 2025 ↗ (opens in a new tab)
